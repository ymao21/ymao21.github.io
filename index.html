Featured projects

  1. Agentic AI · MCP · RAG · Jun–Aug 2026 · Four-person team

    Voice Product Discovery

    A four-person team project: a voice-and-text shopping agent that keeps a shopper's constraints straight across turns and never recommends a product it can't cite. I worked on the Amazon product-data ingestion and Chroma indexing, the LangGraph and MCP integration, retrieval reconciliation and no-match handling, the live-search fallback, and the reliability fixes before the demo.

    Grounding is enforced in code rather than asked for in a prompt: the reconciliation step flags catalog-to-live price gaps above 15%, and a request nothing satisfies returns an explicit no-match instead of an invented product.

  2. Risk Modeling · Business Decisioning · Sep–Dec 2025

    Device Financing Credit Risk

    Roughly 12,000 device-financing applications, framed as an approval decision rather than a classification score. Logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost compared, then the cutoff calibrated on the precision-recall curve.

    Logistic regression won on the balance of ROC-AUC, recall and explainability. The recommended threshold projected about $25.75 in incremental cash flow per applicant.

  3. RAG · Retrieval Evaluation · May 2026

    Research Paper RAG

    Retrieval over 75 arXiv papers built as an experiment: two chunking strategies crossed with three retrieval methods and four prompts, scored with RAGAS plus a deterministic Hit@3 against ground-truth qrels.

    Section-aware chunking with cosine retrieval reached 0.96 faithfulness. The same evaluation found the real ceiling: Hit@3 never passed 0.25, so retrieval rather than generation was capping the system.

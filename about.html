Yining Mao

I grew up in Chengdu, China, and moved to the U.S. for high school in the Greater Boston area. I studied Economics and Math at Boston College and started my career in finance at State Street, working across risk, analytics, algorithmic trading, and automation. The part I liked most was building things, which pulled me toward software engineering, then data science, and eventually a master's in Applied Data Science at the University of Chicago.

Most of what I know I learned by building something badly first. I picked up a Rubik's Cube to see if I could solve it, and then spent weeks shaving seconds off the time. My projects tend to go the same way: the first version works, and then I want to know exactly where it breaks.

When I'm not working, I'm usually outside. I've climbed in Yangshuo, Rumney, and Yosemite, mostly bouldering and top roping, though I still haven't worked up the courage to lead. I play tennis with friends and spend winters skiing or snowboarding wherever I can, from New England and the Rockies to Les Trois Vallées. I travel as much as I can manage, mostly around Europe and Asia, and I'm enough of a foodie that I usually plan a trip around what I want to eat before I work out what I want to see.
